Only if you ask for it

The core idea: change only what you asked for

A good prompt splits the job in two and tells Seedance exactly where the line is. That's what the Prompt Engine is built on.

For example: you have a regular video shot at home → you want it to feel like a professional studio shoot.

CHANGE

Whatever you asked for:

  • outfit
  • interior & background
  • lighting
  • scene details
  • camera angle — on request

KEEP

Everything you didn't ask to change:

  • face & identity
  • hair
  • body & proportions
  • hands
  • movement & gestures
  • facial expressions
  • composition
  • camera & perspective
  • timing

By default, the camera stays exactly as it was. Want a different angle or camera move? Just say so in the prompt.

Writing it yourself

You have to think through and describe all of this on your own:

Using more than one reference

Upload the photos in order and give each one a role, so the model never mixes up what comes from where.

  • @Video1the original video and the person (first image: a screenshot of it)
  • @Image1second image — outfit only
  • @Image2third image — background only

One reference for both the outfit and the background works too. Just say so explicitly — the Prompt Engine will separate the two within the same image.

Common mistakes

Writing “make it look like the photo”

The model won't know what to take. Give it a role: @Image1 — outfit reference or @Image1 — background reference.

Not saying what has to stay

If you don't say the face, the movement and the camera stay the same, Seedance may change those too.

Describing things in general terms

“Beautiful” and “cinematic” aren't instructions. Describe the color, the fabric, the place, the light.
