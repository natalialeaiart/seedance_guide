Only if you ask for it

The core idea: change only what you asked for

The Prompt Engine splits your request in two and tells Seedance exactly where the line is.

For example: you have a regular video shot at home → you want it to feel like a professional studio shoot.

CHANGE

Whatever you asked for:

  • outfit
  • interior & background
  • lighting
  • scene details
  • camera angle — on request

KEEP

Everything you didn't ask to change:

  • face & identity
  • hair
  • body & proportions
  • hands
  • movement & gestures
  • facial expressions
  • composition
  • camera & perspective
  • timing

By default, the camera stays exactly as it was. Want a different angle or camera move? Just say so in your request.

6 steps to your first generation

Steps 1–5 happen in ChatGPT or Claude. Step 6 happens in Seedance 2.5.

  1. 01

    Take a screenshot from your original video

    Pick a frame where the person and the scene are clearly visible. It shows the Prompt Engine the composition and the visual context.

  2. 02

    Prepare a reference photo

    A photo of what you want: the outfit, the interior, a new location or an overall look. One reference can cover the outfit, the background, or both.

    The person in the reference won't replace yours. The face, hair and body always come from your original video.
  3. 03

    Open ChatGPT or Claude and upload 3 files

    • Prompt Engine (PDF)
    • Video screenshot
    • Reference photo
    Order matters: screenshot first, then the reference — that's how the Prompt Engine knows which is which.
    Don't upload the examples PDF — it's for reading only.
  4. 04

    Copy and send this request

    Only replace the text in [square brackets].

    Read the attached Seedance 2.5 instructions and follow them exactly. Write a ready-to-use prompt. @Video1 — the source video; the first image is a screenshot of one of its frames. @Image1 — the reference photo, the second image. Use it for: [the outfit / the background / both]. What it shows: [a woman wrapped in a blanket, in the mountains]. Follow the format and rules in the instructions.
    No need to write a long technical prompt yourself.

    Filled-in example: Use it for: both. What it shows: a woman in a black suit in a modern studio.

  5. 05

    Get your ready-to-use prompt

    The prompt is always in English — that's by design.

    Underneath it, you'll get a short breakdown in the same language you wrote your request in: what comes from the video, what comes from the reference, what changes and what stays.

  6. 06

    Paste the prompt into Seedance 2.5

    Open whichever platform you use for Seedance 2.5. Upload your original video and the reference images, paste in the prompt and hit generate.

    The PDF goes into ChatGPT or Claude only. Seedance just gets the finished prompt.

Using more than one reference

Upload the photos in order and give each one a role, so the model never mixes up what comes from where.

  • @Video1the original video and the person (first image: a screenshot of it)
  • @Image1second image — outfit only
  • @Image2third image — background only

One reference for both the outfit and the background works too. Just say so explicitly — the Prompt Engine will separate the two within the same image.

Common mistakes

Uploading the PDF to Seedance

The Prompt Engine runs in ChatGPT or Claude. Seedance only gets the finished prompt.

Not giving the reference a role

Not “make it look like the photo,” but @Image1 — outfit reference or @Image1 — background reference.

Not saying what has to stay

If keeping the person, their movement or the camera matters to you, make that clear in your request.

Assuming the camera can't change

It stays the same by default. Need a different angle, perspective or camera move? Just ask for it.
